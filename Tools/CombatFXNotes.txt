Niagara effects pass 1 — 2026-10-06

Implemented: hand-aligned melee crescents with brief independent decay; stronger
combo styling; damage-event impact sparks; directional successful-block contact
flash; replacement of the old flat block semicircle/icon; animated wall-torch
flames/embers; existing warm/cold flickering light halos; low-density drifting
perimeter mist. Atmosphere composites before actors and combat effects after them.
Shared Niagara sprite material has separate procedural phases for these effects.
Its particle ceiling is 8 seconds; per-instance cosmetic lifetimes are shorter and
explicitly culled (arrow unchanged at .55s, melee .18/.26s, block .32s, fire .85s,
mist 3.8s). Each of foreground/background queues is capped at 160 components.

No changes to damage, collision, cooldown, block mitigation or gameplay timing.
Lighting is a 2D composited treatment, not dynamic lighting of 3D dungeon geometry.
Enemy/boss-specific attacks, other skill effects and a per-theme art review are
not finished in this first pass. Existing skill/projectile artwork remains intact.
