Grease campaign exclusion (next build)

Grease is temporarily disabled in normal campaign progression. The Forgotten
Keep now descends directly into the military bunker. Remaining bosses retain
their relative order, ending with Iron Matriarch. Visible floor numbers and
balance/loot tiers are consecutive across the seven active floors.

All Grease artwork, enemies, boss, audio, authored IDs and editor test entry
points are retained. No content was deleted. Assets may still be cooked;
this is a gameplay exclusion, not a package-size optimization.

Restore by setting DungeonProgression::IncludeGrease to true and updating
the explicit active-route regression expectations in VerifyAtlas.
This source change does not alter previously packaged or published builds.
