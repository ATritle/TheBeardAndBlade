Arrow effects implementation — 2026-10-06

Runtime: DungeonArrowFX.cpp/h. Dedicated Niagara system and unlit animated material
live in /Game/Effects/Arrows. Existing arrow damage, lifetime, swept collision and
elemental status application are unchanged. The attached canvas sine-wave trail
and old arrow-impact flipbooks are replaced by Niagara release/wake/impact phases.

Each wake particle is emitted at a fixed 18-unit distance interval in a private
orthographic scene plane. It is independent of the arrow after emission, spreads
and fades over 0.55 seconds, and survives projectile impact/destruction. Material
age follows gameplay time, including inventory pause. Components are destroyed
after 0.62 seconds and bounded to 160. Menu, trader and room travel clear effects.
Scene capture uses a show-only particle list and is composited additively before
the HUD. Its 1280x800 logical coordinates follow HUD letterboxing and scale.

Gold/fire/frost/poison/arcane tint and power-shot intensity are per-instance.
Charge remains 1.5 seconds, auto-fires once, and requires release before rearming.

Editor-only BuildArrowNiagara flag regenerates the two assets; this builder uses
UE 5.8 stateless Niagara editor APIs. Runtime does not depend on those editor APIs.
DefaultGame.ini explicitly cooks the effects; package_windows.ps1 audits them.
